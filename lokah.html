Our promises

Built to be trusted.

Lokah is designed around a few rules it can't break. They're written into how it's built, not just into a policy.

Yours alone

Your world runs on its own machine. It's never shared with anyone else's.

Honest memory

Every memory says where it came from. Guesses are always marked as guesses.

Asks first

Anything that books, sends or pays waits for your approval.

Calm, not noisy

Only what earns its place reaches your morning. Fast answers for small things.

No passwords

Sign in with a passkey: your face or fingerprint. Nothing for anyone to steal.

Keys stay locked

The keys to your accounts stay in a locked gate. Lokah itself never holds them in the clear.

Works with the tools you already use

Emails, web pages and documents are read as information, never as instructions, so a stranger's message can't tell your Lokah what to do.
