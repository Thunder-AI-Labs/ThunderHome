Which languages does Yotes speak?

English and Tamil today, including mixed Tamil and English. More languages will follow once native speakers have tested them.

Do I need an account?

No. Notes, reminders and Spaces work fully on your phone without one. Sign in only if you want backup, sync or family sharing.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. This removes your account and everything stored with it on our servers.

Why aren't my reminders ringing?

Check that notifications are allowed for Yotes in your phone's Settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb isn't silencing them.
