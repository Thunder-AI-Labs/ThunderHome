How we build

Every product is different. The standard isn't.

We build many kinds of AI products. Each one ships only when it meets the same four rules.

Rule 1

Yours

Your data stays yours. Private by default, never sold, never mixed with anyone else's.

Rule 2

Honest

Clear about what it knows and what it guesses. No invented facts.

Rule 3

Asks first

Nothing important happens until you tap Confirm or Approve.

Rule 4

Fast and fair

The smallest model that does the job well, so it feels instant and stays affordable.
